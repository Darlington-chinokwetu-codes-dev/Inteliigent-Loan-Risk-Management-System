ABOUT LOANRISK

Risk insights, with people at the center.

LoanRisk provides a structured estimate of loan default risk to support a more informed human review.

How the assessment works

Applicant, loan and credit history information is validated and prepared for a trained machine-learning model. The result presents an estimated default probability, a risk category and supporting indicators.

Model development

Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest and XGBoost.

Performance evaluation

Model performance is assessed using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score and ROC-AUC. The training workflow selects the best-performing model using ROC-AUC.

Responsible use

Results are estimates, not guarantees or automatic lending decisions. Use appropriate, verified data and apply human oversight, privacy safeguards, fairness testing, explainability and all relevant regulatory review before any real-world use.
